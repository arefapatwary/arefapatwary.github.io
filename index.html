About

I am a PhD student at the University of North Texas. My research focuses on large language models, especially mechanistic interpretability, robustness, and fairness and bias. I want to understand what happens inside these models when their answers shift, and to use that understanding to make them more reliable and fair.

Before my PhD, I earned an MS in Artificial Intelligence from the University of North Texas and a BS in Computer Science and Engineering from North South University in Dhaka, Bangladesh. I also worked in industry as a software engineer and data analyst, building and deploying machine learning systems.

Research

Prompt Robustness Is Task-Dependent: Comparing Objective and Belief-Style Questions in LLM Evaluation

Sadia Kamal, Arefa Patwary, Anthony Marchiafava, Sagnik Ray Choudhury, Atriya Sen

arXiv preprint arXiv:2607.05554, 2026

We test the consistency of the LLM's output when a question is reworded, reformatted, or reframed,or even the labels are substituted with different symbols, across objective benchmarks and belief-style survey datasets. Robustness turns out to depend jointly on the model, the type of question, and the kind of prompt change, with belief-style questions being less stable.
